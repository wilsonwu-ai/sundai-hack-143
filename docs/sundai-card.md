**Title**

Movement Age

**One-line preview** (result first)

Your phone counts your chair stands and shows which age group you move like, from a published table of 393 adults aged 18 to 80. About 2 minutes, nothing uploaded.

**Description**

Try it: https://wilsonwu-ai.github.io/sundai-hack-143/

What it does: prop up your phone, do 5 chair stands and a one-leg stand. On-device pose tracking (MediaPipe) counts the stands and times the balance, then places your result on the age table of a 2025 reference study (Colombia Medica: 393 adults, ages 18 to 80).

Why it works: reps are counted from your hip height relative to your own torso, so it works from the front or the side, at any distance. And balance matters: adults aged 51 to 75 who could not hold a one-leg stand for 10 seconds had an 84% higher risk of death during follow-up (BJSM 2022).

Limits: an age band, never a single number; the table comes from one country; not a medical device.

Code and evidence trail, with every number quote-checked against its source: https://github.com/wilsonwu-ai/sundai-hack-143
